# SeeForMe AI 👁️

### Your Eyes. Your AI. Your Independence.

SeeForMe AI is an AI-powered visual assistant designed to help visually impaired and blind individuals interact with their surroundings more independently and confidently.

Powered by Google's Gemini AI, SeeForMe AI transforms visual information into understandable descriptions and natural voice guidance, making everyday activities more accessible.

---

## 🌟 The Problem

For visually impaired individuals, everyday tasks such as identifying objects, reading documents, recognizing surroundings, and navigating unfamiliar environments can be challenging.

Access to real-time visual information is essential for independence, but traditional assistive solutions may not always provide the flexibility and context users need.

## 💡 Our Solution

SeeForMe AI uses Google's Gemini AI to provide intelligent visual assistance through a simple and accessible interface.

Users can capture their surroundings and receive AI-generated descriptions, document-reading assistance, and spoken guidance to better understand the world around them.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI-Powered Visual Assistance:** Understand surroundings through intelligent image analysis.
- **Object Recognition:** Identify and describe objects in the user's environment.
- **Document Reading:** Extract and understand text from captured documents.
- **Voice Assistance:** Interact through speech recognition and spoken AI responses.
- **Multilingual Support:** English, Urdu, and Pashto support.
- **RTL Support:** Proper right-to-left layouts for Urdu and Pashto.
- **Accessibility Settings:** High-contrast mode and adjustable text sizes.
- **Speech Controls:** Adjustable speech rate for a comfortable experience.
- **Screen Reader Optimization:** Accessibility-focused interface improvements.

## 🛠️ Technology

- Google Gemini AI
- Google AI Studio
- AI-powered image understanding
- Speech recognition and audio responses
